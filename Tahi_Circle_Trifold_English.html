INSIDE • STUDIO ACCESS

3 Using the studio

Orientation comes first

Receive guidance before using machines or tools. Ask for help with unfamiliar equipment.

Two projects as your contribution

Complete two agreed projects you have already been taught. You may use studio equipment to make them during scheduled sessions.

The contribution belongs to the studio

Once completed and accepted, the two projects become Tahi Circle’s property. They may be used as samples, displayed, or sold as agreed.

Agree on the access you receive

Record the project type and size, who supplies materials, quality expectations, and the sessions or access period covered. No additional contribution without prior discussion.

Practice your own projects

After your contribution, choose personal projects within your skills, equipment availability, and agreed access. Your own creations belong to you unless otherwise agreed.

Fabric and other materials

You may request fabric; confirm availability, price, and payment terms. If unavailable, bring fabric bought elsewhere after a suitability check. Confirm charges for thread, zippers, lining, batting, and other consumables.

INSIDE • PAY AFTER SALE

4 Create now,
pay after sale

Record materials before taking them

Record the type, quantity, price, and intended project for all precut fabrics taken. Make projects you have already been taught. Every withdrawal must be recorded.

Make multiple projects

There is no fixed project limit, subject to available materials, equipment, and the agreed schedule.

Pay for fabric after the sale

Pay the agreed price of the fabric actually used after the product sells. Fabric payment is not yet due while the product remains unsold.

10% of profit for equipment upkeep

In addition to fabric payment, contribute 10% of profit for repairs and maintenance. Confirm how profit is calculated before starting.

Report sales and payments

Follow the agreed remittance schedule. Report deposits, installments, cancellations, or refunds so records can be updated.

Take care of materials

Return usable leftover fabric and update the records. Discuss payment first if an item will be kept for personal use or given as a gift.

Example using agreed net profit:
₱300 sale - ₱100 fabric - ₱50 other direct costs = ₱150 profit.
10% contribution = ₱15.
Pay the studio: ₱115 including fabric.

INSIDE • SHARED STUDIO

5 Care for our circle

Book ahead and share

Confirm your visit. Workspace and equipment are shared; access is neither exclusive nor unlimited.

Sew safely

Tie back long hair and keep hands clear of needles. Switch machines off before changing needles or making adjustments. Report jams and faults; no unauthorized repairs.

Practice and responsibility

Ask for guidance before cutting or trying new techniques. Purchased practice fabric is part of your costs. Report damage; its cause will be reviewed before any charge.

Clean up before leaving

Return tools, collect pins and needles, and sort scraps. Keep food and drinks away from work areas. Switch off machines and unplug irons.

Respect and privacy

No bullying or harassment. Ask before using or moving another person’s work, taking photos, or sharing customer details.

If you stop participating

Tell the coordinator and discuss unfinished work and materials. Personal belongings and products do not automatically become studio property because of absence or unsold items.

Follow studio guidance

Sessions or access may be paused for unsafe or repeatedly disruptive behavior.

These are separate arrangements: the two-project contribution for studio access and the 10% profit contribution on pay-after-sale projects. Clarify both during orientation.
