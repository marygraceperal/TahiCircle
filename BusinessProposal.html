Every creation begins with fabric

Tahi Circle is a fabric business with a shared sewing space and a community that learns together. Here, you can choose fabrics for your project, exchange ideas, and practice alongside fellow sewing enthusiasts.

Sewing machines and tools may be used during arranged sessions, subject to availability, guidance, and studio rules. Projects made using our facilities use fabrics purchased from Tahi Circle.

You do not need to be an expert. Start with a project that interests you and build your skills gradually. Participation is optional; suitable ways to take part are discussed during orientation.

What you can explore

Choose your fabric

Explore colors and prints that suit your idea. Discuss suitable fabrics and materials before starting your project.

Learn with others

Observe, ask questions, share what you know, and practice according to your interests and the guidance available.

Create something of your own

Explore pillow covers, pouches, bags, table linens, patchwork, and other sewn items. Choose a project that suits your skills.

Explore opportunities to earn

For those interested in selling, handmade creations can become products. You manage your own sales and communicate with your customers.

Create for personal use, gifts, or selling. Sales and income are not guaranteed, but there is an opportunity to learn and improve your craft.

Start with an idea

  1. Share what you want to make. Tell us what you would like to learn or create.
  2. Attend orientation. Get to know the studio, equipment, and ways to participate.
  3. Discuss your project. Choose your fabric and clarify the materials, schedule, and applicable arrangements.
  4. Learn and create. Begin your agreed activity alongside the community.

Get to know our circle

Orientation covers participation, machine and tool use, safety guidance, schedules, material prices and payment arrangements, and any applicable fees or agreements.

You will have an opportunity to ask questions and clarify the details before deciding, purchasing, or using the facilities. This page introduces the studio; arrangements for individual projects are discussed separately.
