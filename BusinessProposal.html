Tahi Circle Customer Proposal

A shared space for fabric customers to learn and create

Learn together. Sew together. Sell together. Grow together.

An invitation to create with us

Tahi Circle invites interested ones to purchase project fabrics, learn alongside fellow makers, and use shared sewing machines and tools during arranged studio sessions. You can make items for personal use or develop products to sell independently.

Our main business is selling fabrics. Our community brings customers together to exchange ideas, practice sewing, and support one another. Participation is optional, and income depends on your products, pricing, costs, and ability to find buyers.

What you can access

Project fabrics and sewing supplies at prices disclosed before purchase or use.

Peer learning and shared practice based on participants’ interests and available support.

Sewing machines and selected tools during confirmed sessions, subject to availability and safe use.

A supportive setting for planning, making, and discussing products such as pouches, bags, pillow covers, and other sewn items.

How to get started

Tell us what you want to make and discuss the skills, materials, and equipment needed.

Confirm the material quantities, prices, payment option, and any applicable studio support fee before cutting.

Arrange a studio session, learn the equipment rules, and create your project.

Keep your finished item or sell it yourself. Set your own price and manage your customers.

For a qualifying sale, settle outstanding project materials and the agreed studio support fee under your project terms.

You do not need to promise regular attendance or submit a withdrawal letter. Stopping attendance does not cancel an existing purchase agreement.

Purchases and Studio Support

Fabrics for studio projects

Projects made using Tahi Circle’s facilities use fabrics purchased from Tahi Circle. This condition applies to studio projects; it does not restrict what you buy or make elsewhere. Ask about any additional supplies you need before starting.

Fabric used for practice, cutting trials, or unsuccessful sewing attempts remains part of your purchase. There is no additional damage penalty for fabric you have already bought. Replacement materials are a separate purchase. Any concern involving defective equipment or studio guidance should be reviewed before additional charges are agreed.

Choose your material payment option

Option What to agree before use
Pay upfront Confirm the material price and pay before starting. This is suitable for practice and personal-use projects.
Pay after sale by prior agreement Available only when expressly approved for a particular project. Record the material price and the sale-payment trigger. If the agreement says payment is due only after a sale, there is no deadline while the item remains unsold.
Another agreed schedule Any installment or alternative schedule must be recorded before materials are used.

For pay-after-sale projects, agree when payment must be remitted after customer funds are received, how deposits and refunds are handled, and what happens if the item is kept, gifted, damaged, or abandoned. Materials for practice that will not be sold need a separate payment arrangement.

A contribution from qualifying sales

Tahi Circle proposes a studio support fee on sales of products made using its facilities. This helps meet electricity bills, machine repairs, maintenance, and shared supply costs. The percentage is not yet fixed and must be disclosed and accepted before a project begins.

Proposed calculation: studio support fee = agreed percentage × actual product selling price after discounts, excluding separately charged delivery. Fabric charges are separate. A project record must state whether the fee replaces any session charge so that no unexpected additional fee is imposed.

For personal-use or practice projects, confirm any studio-access charge before booking. A sale-based fee alone does not explain access costs for items that will never be sold.

Your creations and earnings

You own and sell your creations, choose customers and prices, and handle delivery and customer concerns. Your remaining proceeds are the sale payment less material purchases, the agreed support fee, and your other expenses. These proceeds still need to compensate your time and labor. Inactivity does not automatically transfer your products to the studio or other participants.

A respectful shared studio
